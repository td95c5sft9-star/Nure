Signs it is time for an eye test

Some eye conditions have no early symptoms, so regular tests matter. Book a visit if you notice any of these.

Frequent headaches
Blurred or double vision
Tired, strained eyes after screen time
Difficulty seeing at night
Squinting to read or see far away
A child who sits close to screens or squints
